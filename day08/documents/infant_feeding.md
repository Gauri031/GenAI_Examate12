# Infant and Young Child Feeding (SAMPLE)

## Breastfeeding

Breast milk is the best food for a baby. For the first 6 months give only breast milk. A baby does not need water, honey, ghutti or any other food or drink during this time. Start breastfeeding within one hour of birth and feed whenever the baby shows hunger, day and night.

Continue breastfeeding after 6 months, up to 2 years or more, along with other foods.

## Starting complementary food at 6 months

At 6 months breast milk alone is no longer enough, so start soft home-cooked foods. Good weaning foods are mashed dal and rice, khichdi with a little ghee or oil, ragi porridge, mashed banana, mashed boiled potato or pumpkin, and well-cooked mashed egg.

Start with 2 to 3 spoons once or twice a day and slowly increase the amount. At 6 to 8 months give 2 to 3 meals a day. At 9 to 11 months give 3 to 4 meals plus 1 to 2 snacks. Introduce one new food at a time and wait a few days before the next.

## What to avoid in the first year

Do not add salt or sugar to the baby's food. Do not give honey before 1 year of age. Do not give cow's or buffalo milk as the main drink before 12 months. Avoid tea, packaged snacks and whole nuts, which can cause choking. Always wash hands and use clean utensils.
