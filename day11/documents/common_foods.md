# Common Indian Foods: Quick Facts (SAMPLE)

## Energy and sweeteners

One medium roti made from about 30 g of wheat flour gives roughly 100 kcal. It gives more if ghee or oil is added. A small bowl of cooked rice or dal gives about 100 to 150 kcal.

Jaggery contains a small amount of iron, but it is still mostly sugar. It is not a treatment for anaemia, and the amount eaten is too small to replace iron-rich foods or iron tablets. Use sweets in small amounts for children.

## Vegetables rich in vitamin A

Good, easily available choices are drumstick leaves, spinach and other green leafy vegetables, pumpkin, carrot and ripe papaya. Prices change by season and place, so check your local market for the cheapest seasonal option.
