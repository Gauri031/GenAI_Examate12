# Nutrition for Pregnant and Breastfeeding Women (SAMPLE)

## Eating well in pregnancy

A pregnant woman needs more food than usual because the baby is growing, especially in the second and third trimesters. Eat a variety of foods every day: cereals like roti and rice, dal and pulses, milk and curd, eggs if you eat them, green leafy vegetables, other vegetables and seasonal fruits. Eat small meals more often if a large meal causes discomfort.

In the second trimester appetite usually improves and the baby grows fast. Add one extra meal or snack such as a bowl of dal, a glass of milk, roasted chana, groundnuts or a fruit. Take the iron and folic acid tablets and any calcium tablets exactly as given by your Anganwadi or health worker.

## Preventing anaemia

Anaemia is common in women and can cause tiredness, pale skin and breathlessness. Eat iron-rich foods such as green leafy vegetables, lentils, dates, ragi and, if you eat them, eggs and meat or fish. Eat something rich in vitamin C, such as lemon, amla, guava or orange, with the meal because it helps the body absorb iron. Avoid drinking tea or coffee with meals because it reduces iron absorption.

## After delivery

A breastfeeding mother needs extra food and plenty of water or other fluids. Continue the iron and calcium tablets if the health worker advises. Eat a variety of foods and do not avoid dal, vegetables, fruits or milk products.
